REPEAT Boundary and Archive Contract

Finalized decisions and immediate actions

Architectural boundary

REPEAT_CORE
Purpose: technical verification and replay
Authority: governed by REPEAT_CORE rules
Input: technical evidence and state artifacts
Output: technical verification result

HUMAN_RESEARCH_RECORD
Purpose: preserve human-originated history
Authority: historical and provenance only
Input: observations, hypotheses, actions, interpretations
Output: durable historical record

PROVENANCE
Purpose: establish relationships between records and artifacts
Authority: none over technical truth
Input: identifiers, timestamps, authorship, hashes, relationships
Output: traceability

ARCHIVE
Purpose: preserve a reproducible snapshot
Authority: none
Input: REPEAT_CORE + HUMAN_RESEARCH_RECORD + PROVENANCE
Output: portable historical artifact

Explicit invariant

PROVENANCE_PRESERVATION != AUTHORITY_TRANSFER

The existence of a provenance record must never, by itself, grant its contents technical authority.

Core invariants

P1: Preservation does not imply truth.
P2: Attribution does not imply correctness.
P3: Chronology does not imply causality.
P4: Interpretation does not become observation.
P5: A provenance record cannot modify REPEAT_CORE authority.
P6: A hash establishes artifact identity and integrity, not semantic truth.
P7: Retraction preserves historical existence; it does not erase the record.
P8: UNRESOLVED remains UNRESOLVED unless an authorized process changes status.
P9: Archive completeness must not depend on ChatGPT, Copilot, GitHub, or any single platform.
P10: The historical record must remain intelligible without the originating AI system.

Decision

The archive itself is the door.

  • Human experience becomes observation, thought, experiment, and revision.
  • That history is preserved in a durable artifact with identifiers, provenance, evidence, and continuity.
  • The archive does not decide whether the person was right.
  • It prevents the work from disappearing because no one preserved the path by which it came into existence.

Testable requirement

Given the archive five, fifty, or one hundred years from now, can an independent person reconstruct what was observed, hypothesized, attempted, what happened, what changed, and which artifacts support each step without asking the original AI?

  • If yes: PRESERVED.
  • If no: NOT PRESERVED.

Immediate actions

  • Write the boundary and authority contract into the architecture documentation.
  • Define the authoritative status model for REPEAT_CORE and the historical record.
  • Implement archive-level provenance metadata and integrity rules.
  • Require reconstructability over time as a design and review test.
  • Commit to the archive as the durable door between lived experience and future investigation.

Conclusion

This is not a claim about sentience, immortality, or ultimate meaning. It is a testable preservation requirement: preserve the path of inquiry so that future investigators can understand what happened and why it matters without depending on the originating system.
