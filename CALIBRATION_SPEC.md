# CALIBRATION_SPEC

This document defines the calibration invariants and thresholds that must be adhered to during the calibration processes. 

## Invariants
1. **Temperature**: Calibration must be performed at a constant temperature of 20°C ± 2°C.
2. **Humidity**: Humidity levels should remain within 30% to 50%.
3. **Equipment**: Use only certified measurement devices that have been calibrated within the last 12 months.

## Thresholds
- Measurement tolerances should not exceed ± 0.5% for standard measurements.
- Calibration drift should be checked and corrected if the drift exceeds the threshold of ± 1%.
